## Design Specificity Verdict — correction to Assessment A

Assessment A judged this design as authored for Afoogy specifically, citing (among other things) "cash/bank/mobile-money-shaped payment methods with no 'Pay now' flow." I checked this directly and it's not accurate: a `grep` across all 42 boards for "mobile money," "momo," "m-pesa," and "mpesa" returns **zero matches anywhere in the design**. What the boards actually show is **Card**, **Bank transfer**, and **Cash** — "Card" appears as a payment method 4 times across the Finance boards alone, and dominates the "Collected by method" chart at 52% of volume, more than cash and bank transfer combined.

This matters because cash-and-mobile-money (explicitly *not* card/online-gateway) is called out in the product's own positioning as the thing a generic competitor "could not truthfully claim" — it's the single most product-specific fact about how this business actually takes money, and the design shows the opposite: a payment method (Card) that the product has explicitly never supported anywhere, dominating the example data, while the actual differentiator never appears once. Net effect on the verdict: still clearly authored for Afoogy on role model, status taxonomy, and document design — but this is a real, specific instance of the design reaching for generic-SaaS defaults exactly where the brief most needed it not to.

## Overall Impression

This is a genuinely disciplined, coherent design system — not a pile of disconnected screens. The seven-tone status taxonomy, the reversal/correction pattern, and the published-vs-closed banner distinction are implemented at the token/CSS level with real rigor, confirmed by both assessments independently. The single biggest problem is not a visual one: it's that the design's approval-queue component — reused everywhere self-approval could occur — hard-codes a rule (self-approval blocked) that the product's own documentation says is no longer correct for Expenses, and may no longer be correct for Results and Grade Configs either. That one decision, repeated structurally rather than as a one-off slip, is the thing to fix before anyone builds from these boards.

## What's Working

1. **The slate/violet/gray status system is real, not just documented.** `Theme.dc.html` defines distinct hex tokens and a distinct icon per state (archive for closed, lock for published, pencil for draft), and it's applied correctly everywhere both assessments checked — Published (`bn-pub`, violet, lock) and Closed (`bn-cls`, slate, archive, hatched texture) never read as the same thing.
2. **The reversal/correction pattern is implemented identically everywhere money or a result can go wrong** — academy payments, platform subscription payments, exam results. Original record always kept (struck-through, "Reversed"), new linked record created, reason always required, no native browser `prompt()`/`confirm()`/`alert()` anywhere in any of the 42 boards.
3. **Paper documents correctly stay light in dark mode at the CSS level**, not just by convention: `.paper,.cert,.idc,.rcpt` (plus `@media print`) re-pin the full light token set regardless of the ancestor theme, so this can't regress even under a dark shell.
4. **Owner-only Platform capabilities use two different, both-correct patterns for two different affordance types** — a static "Owner only, can't be granted" badge in the permission-grant editor (stronger than a disabled toggle), and a visibly-disabled button with a tooltip for in-context actions (Reactivate, Add staff). Both match the "never a toggle, never silently hidden" rule.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Self-approval is hard-blocked everywhere it can occur — and the root cause traces back to the brief I wrote you, not just the design tool.**
Why it matters: both assessments independently found this in the shared approval-queue component (`P1-Components.dc.html`), the Exam Results queue (`P4-Review.dc.html`), the Expenses queue (`P5-Receipts.dc.html`), the mobile approvals screen (`P8-MobileA.dc.html`), and — Assessment B's catch — a dedicated, permanently-disabled Settings toggle at `P6-Settings.dc.html:72` reading "Self-approval is never allowed." `afoogy-design/BRIEF.md` itself states the absolute rule "nobody approves their own submission." On checking `CLAUDE_DESIGN_PROMPT.md` (the prompt I generated for you earlier in this session), line 211 instructs: "...with exactly two exceptions: self-approval of one's own submission, and capabilities permanently restricted to the Platform Owner role. In both cases, show the control in a visible, disabled state..." — and line 213 compounds it by saying self-approval "is allowed" in the same sentence that says to render it via "the permission-locked control pattern" (which is, by the prompt's own definition, the disabled-with-tooltip pattern). That's a direct, self-contradictory instruction I wrote, and Claude Design followed the disabled-state half of it faithfully and consistently. The actual, current product rule (per `DESIGN.md` §11.4) is that Expenses self-approval is allowed outright for Owner/Admin/Manager/Finance Officer, and Results/Grade-Configs likely are too (flagged in `DESIGN.md` itself as "potentially stale, not yet independently verified" — code evidence in `results.ts`/`grade-configurations.ts` test suites suggests self-approval is allowed there as well).
Fix: correct `CLAUDE_DESIGN_PROMPT.md` lines 211 and 213 first (remove self-approval from the disabled-with-tooltip exception list entirely), then either regenerate or hand-patch the 6 affected boards and the Settings toggle.

**[P0] The Platform Staff page is fully visible to a `platform_admin`, when the authoritative spec says it must never appear for that role at all, regardless of grants.**
Where: `P7-PlatformStaff.dc.html`, screen 3 ("Same screen — viewer: Priya Okafor, Billing Manager"). `DESIGN.md` §4.1 is unambiguous: "**Plans** and **Platform Staff** never appear for any `platform_admin`, regardless of grants" — staff/grant management is permanently owner-only. The board shows Priya (a Billing Manager / `platform_admin`) looking at the full Platform Staff list, with only the "Add platform staff" button disabled-with-tooltip. Per the rule, this entire page should be absent from her navigation — not reachable with one button grayed out.
Fix: remove this screen variant's premise, or reframe it as "what happens if a `platform_admin` guesses the URL" with a permission-denied screen instead of a read-only staff list.

**[P1] A payment method ("Card") that the product has never supported appears repeatedly and dominates the example data, while "mobile money" — the stated #1 market differentiator — never appears once.**
Where: `P5-Finance.dc.html` (dashboard "Collected by method" chart, Payments table), `P5-Receipts.dc.html` (the receipt card itself). See the Design Specificity section above for the full evidence.
Fix: replace "Card" with "Mobile money" (or "Cash / Mobile money / Bank transfer," matching `DESIGN.md`'s own method list) everywhere a payment method is shown as an example value.

**[P1] No ARIA tabs pattern on any of the 137 `.tab` button instances across the design.**
Why it matters: tabs are plain buttons in a `<div class="tabs">` with no `role="tablist"`/`role="tab"`/`aria-selected`. Keyboard-focusable (they're real buttons) but screen-reader users get no "tab 2 of 7, selected" semantics, and arrow-key navigation (expected by the standard pattern) is absent. WCAG 4.1.2.
Fix: add the ARIA tabs pattern to the shared `.tabs`/`.tab` component definition once; it propagates everywhere.

**[P1] Certificate issuance has no confirmation step, despite being effectively irreversible and publicly visible — asymmetric with Cancel, which gets a full two-step flow.**
Where: `P6-Certificates.dc.html` — Issue is a single-click form modal; Cancel is reason-then-confirm. A certificate can only be undone by cancelling (which publicly marks it "Cancelled" on the verify page) and reissuing, so the harder-to-undo action currently has less friction than the easy, record-preserving one.
Fix: add a lightweight "Issue certificate for {student}?" confirmation step to match the weight Cancel already gets.

**[P2] The locked branch-selector icon is inconsistent — a location-pin on 3 boards where every other board uses a lock.**
Where: `P6-IdCards.dc.html`, `P8-MobileA.dc.html`, `P8-Tablet.dc.html` use `#i-pin` for the same `.brsel.lk` control that uses `#i-lock` everywhere else (`P1-Shells.dc.html`, `P3-Admissions.dc.html`, `P4-MarkEntry.dc.html`); `P6-IdCards.dc.html`'s own icon sprite doesn't even define `#i-lock`. The whole point of this control is to signal "you cannot change this" to branch-limited roles — a pin reads as an ordinary, clickable location selector, undermining that signal precisely on the boards (ID Cards, Mobile) where Admissions Officers are most likely to be looking at it.
Fix: swap the icon and add the missing symbol def on the 3 affected boards.

**[P2] The single highest-stakes page in the product (Finance) shows 3–4 competing "primary" money actions at once.**
Where: `P5-Finance.dc.html` — the page header offers "Create charge" + "Record payment"; switching to the Charges tab adds a separately-styled second "Create charge" button next to Export in the table toolbar.
Fix: keep page-level primary actions in the header only; demote or remove the toolbar-level duplicate.

**[P2] Touch-target sizing fix exists but was applied to only 2 of roughly 13 icon buttons on the Mobile/Tablet boards.**
Where: `P8-MobileA.dc.html`/`P8-MobileB.dc.html`'s Back button and 7 of 9 rail icons on `P8-Tablet.dc.html` stay at the CSS default 40×40px, while 2 sibling controls on the same Tablet board were explicitly bumped to 44×44px. The fix is a known, applied pattern — just not rolled out consistently.
Fix: apply the same `44×44` override to the remaining icon buttons on those 3 boards.

**[P2] A handful of hard-coded colors live outside the token set in Platform chrome.**
Where: `afoogy.css:225-233` (`.plat .hd .srch .inp` and `.ptag` use raw `rgba(255,255,255,...)`), `afoogy.css:205` (`.logo::after`), the QR module colors. Mostly defensible (the Platform header is deliberately always-navy; QR is inherently black/white) but not expressed as custom properties, so a future palette change won't propagate here automatically.
Fix: promote these to named tokens even if their value never changes with theme.

**[P3] No keyboard-shortcut affordance anywhere, despite Mark Entry and Approvals being daily-use, repetitive workflows that would benefit most from one.**
**[P3] One real contrast miss (4.2:1 vs. the 4.5:1 floor) in `Theme.dc.html`'s own token-reference table** — confined to documentation, not a live surface, but worth fixing since it's the project's own reference.
**[P3] Decorative icons paired with visible text aren't marked `aria-hidden="true"`** — low practical risk, still worth the one-line fix at the shared icon-usage level.
**[P3 / minor]** The Platform Owner is named inconsistently across boards ("Marcus Reid" in `P1-Shells.dc.html`/`P2-PlatformDashboard.dc.html` vs. "Dana Reyes" everywhere in Phase 7) — cosmetic, but visible once the boards are stitched into one prototype.
**[P3 / minor]** Several `i-shield` icon paths use `3.500`/`4.500` instead of `3.5`/`4.5` — harmless, but suggests a find-replace artifact in the tool chain worth a quick check elsewhere.

## Business-Rule Compliance (full checklist)

**Confirmed correct, no action needed:**
- Approve/Reject/Publish modeled as genuinely separate actions; Reject always requires a typed reason via a real modal — zero native `prompt()`/`confirm()`/`alert()` anywhere in 42 boards.
- No delete button exists on any posted financial or result record — only Reversal/Correction, which preserves the original (struck-through, "Reversed"/"Corrected") and creates a new linked record. The only "delete" affordances anywhere are on unpublished drafts, which is correct.
- Branch-limited roles get a locked branch label, never "All branches" — confirmed with zero exceptions across 20 files using a branch selector (modulo the icon-consistency issue above).
- Published (violet/lock) and Closed (slate/archive) banners are visually and textually distinct everywhere both patterns appear.
- The public certificate-verification page shows zero academy-status information in any of its 4 variants (valid/cancelled/not-found/phone-width) — confirmed clean.
- Academy and Platform consoles are structurally separate nav trees with distinct shell styling, never a blended switcher.
- Nothing in any board implies attendance tracking, student self-service login, online/automated payment checkout, or bulk/CSV import — confirmed by grep (the only "attendance" hits are explicit "this doesn't exist" disclaimers; "CSV" only ever appears as an export, never an import).
- Owner-only Platform capabilities (Suspend, Reactivate, Manage platform staff) are correctly rendered absent-for-non-owner or disabled-with-tooltip, never a silent toggle.

**Confirmed violations (see Priority Issues above for detail):**
- Self-approval blocked where it should be allowed (Expenses confirmed by `DESIGN.md`; Results/Grade-Configs likely, flagged as unconfirmed in `DESIGN.md` itself).
- Platform Staff page reachable by a `platform_admin` (should be absent entirely).
- "Card" payment method shown where none exists in the real product; "mobile money" absent everywhere.

**Open question, not a confirmed violation:** `P7-PlatformStaff.dc.html`'s permission-grant screen offers a single "Record subscription payments" toggle with no separate "Reverse payments" toggle, and no board shows a non-owner Platform role looking at the Payments list to confirm Reverse is actually absent for them. Worth confirming with the product owner rather than reporting as fact.
